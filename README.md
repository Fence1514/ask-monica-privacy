# ask-monica-privacyPrivacy Policy for Ask Monica
Effective Date: November 11, 2026

1. Overview
Ask Monica ("we," "our," or "us") provides an AI-powered assistant designed to help authorized team members access Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), FAQs, and operational guidance.

2. Information We Collect
- Account & Usage Information: Basic authentication details (such as name or work email) and chat prompts submitted to generate responses.
- Technical Diagnostics: Basic device and crash logs used solely to maintain app stability and security.

3. How We Use Information
We use collected information strictly to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the Ask Monica assistant.
- Authenticate authorized users and protect internal company information.
- Troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Data Sharing & AI Processing
We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties. Prompts submitted in the app are processed securely through our AI service providers solely to generate responses and are not used to train public third-party models.

5. Data Security & Retention
We implement industry-standard encryption and access controls to safeguard your data. Information is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.
